The story of New York 2026

From Oakleigh
to New York

In 2026 a squad of young Australians, led by PFL coaches Tom Reilly and Alex Kang, pulled on the green and gold and took on the world. This is their journey.

01The preparation 02Match day 03The World Cup 04Friends forever 05Medals & memories
01

The preparation

Every game started in a huddle. Tom and Alex brought the same detail and intensity they bring to every PFL session in Oakleigh: clear roles, tactics on the whiteboard and belief in every player.

"Clear roles. Play with courage. Look after each other."
02

Match day

Against teams from around the world, our players showed exactly what PFL training is built for: speed, skill and courage on the ball.

Swipe for more →

03

Living the World Cup

Off the pitch, the squad and their families were swept up in FIFA World Cup 2026: a sea of gold for Australia vs Paraguay, and electric nights in Times Square.

04

Friends forever

Rivals on the pitch, friends off it. Shirts were swapped and signed, new mates were made from the Netherlands to Brazil, and the squad came home closer than ever.

Same dream. Different flags.

05

Medals & memories

Every player came home with a medal around their neck, and memories that will last a lifetime.

New York was just the beginning.
