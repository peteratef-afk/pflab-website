Coaching team

Coached by experts

Led by a former professional footballer and coaches with elite European academy experience, our team demands high standards and care about every player's development.

Head Coach · Elite Player Development Coach

Tom Reilly

Tom spent fifteen years inside England's professional academy system, from the age of 6 to 21. He came through the academies at Liverpool FC, where he was signed from U8 to U12, and Blackburn Rovers before earning a full-time scholarship and then a professional contract at Accrington Stanley.

His senior career took him to Sweden with Eskilstuna City in the second division, then to the Welsh Premier League with Bangor City and Cefn Druids, both of which qualified for the UEFA Europa League. He went on to win the Northern Premier League title in English non-league football before moving to Australia.

That journey shapes every PFL session. Tom brings the standards, intensity and attention to detail of English professional academies to Melbourne, and he knows from the inside what it takes for a young player to make the next step: the habits, the work rate and the decisions under pressure.

Today Tom works with boys and girls across all NPL age groups. In the last 12 months alone he has individually coached more than 20 players now competing at NPL1 level, with many more developing across the NPL system.

"My focus is in the details. I break the game down, work on the small margins and mould every player for maximum potential and output, on and off the pitch."Tom Reilly

Aran Donnelly

Aran is a former Lead Coach at one of Europe's largest 1-to-1 and small-group football coaching organisations, with extensive experience developing players from elite youth academy level through to senior professional football.

He has worked with players from some of Europe's leading academy environments, including Manchester City, Benfica, AFC Bournemouth and Stade de Reims, as well as U15 to U21 international players. His senior player experience includes Andrew Omobamidele, Killian Phillips, Corrie Ndaba and Bosun Lawal, all competing across the top levels of European football.

FAQ

    
Questions

    

      
What ages do you coach?
Our academy squads are U7 to U13, with entry by trial. Private 1:1, small-group and group sessions are open to a wider range of ages. Tell us your player's age in the enquiry form and we'll recommend the right option.

      
Does my child need to be at a club?
No. Private and group training is open to players from any club, or no club at all. Many of our players train with us alongside their club football.

      
How do academy trials work?
Register through the enquiry form and select "Academy trial". We'll be in touch with trial details. The academy is designed for ambitious players who enjoy being challenged and want to train with intensity, three days per week.

      
Is training affected by the weather?
No. All sessions run in our indoor facility at 144-154 Haughton Road, Oakleigh, so training goes ahead all year round, rain, hail or heat.

      
What should my child bring?
Indoor or turf-appropriate football boots or trainers, shin pads, a full water bottle and comfortable training clothes.

      
Can I hire the facility?
Yes. Our pitches, gym and speed lane are available for games, personal training, birthday parties and club, school or corporate events. Enquire by WhatsApp on 0421 441 200 or email info@pflab.com.au.

      
How do I book a session?
Send an enquiry through this website, or call or text Coach Tom on 0416 121 596. We'll confirm a time that suits.
