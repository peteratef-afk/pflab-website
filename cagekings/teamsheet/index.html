Submit your teamsheet

One teamsheet per team, due 3 days before your event (Launch Cup: Thursday 3 December, 8pm). List every player who may take the court, with their date of birth and contact details. We check eligibility on the day against this sheet, so please make sure it is complete and correct.

Age groups go by birth year

Go by the year of birth shown next to each age group (U12 is born 2015). Age groups roll over every 1 January; the Launch Cup uses 2027 age groups. Choose the age group your team registered in. Younger players can always play up.

One dispensation per squad

Each squad may include one player who is one year older than the team's age group. Never more than one year.

Girls may play down one year

Girls may play one age group below their own. This does not use your squad's dispensation.
