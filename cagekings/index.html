The rules

Walls in. No offside.

Walls and ceiling in play

Use the walls. The ball is only out when it goes into the back net.

No offside

Play it forward anytime. Pressure never stops.

Keeper by hand

Restarts by hand within 5 seconds. The throw must land in the keeper's own half.

No slide tackles

Stay on your feet. No back-pass pick-ups for keepers.

Direct free kicks

Wall 3 metres back. Serious fouls earn a 2-minute sin bin.

Draws settled fast

Knockout games level at full time go straight to a penalty shoot-out.

FAQ

Questions

Who runs Cage Kings?

Cage Kings is run by Prodigy Futbol Lab, a high-performance football academy in Oakleigh, Melbourne.

How big is a squad?

Every division plays 5v5 with rolling substitutions. Teams need at least 5 players, with no maximum squad size.

How do I pay for the Launch Cup?

Register your team on this page. The bank transfer details appear as soon as you submit, and we'll email you a confirmation shortly after. Payment is due by Sunday 29 November at 8pm. Entry is refunded in full only if a division is called off.

How are prizes paid?

Prize money is paid to the winning team on the day.

Where are the sessions?

The Launch Cup is at Moorabbin Indoor Sports, 2/117 Cochranes Rd, Moorabbin. League venues for 2027 will be announced soon. Grand Finals are held at Prodigy Futbol Lab, 144-154 Haughton Road, Oakleigh.

Can I play without a team?

Yes. Register as an individual player and we'll help place you in a team in your division.
