Privacy policy

How Prodigy Futbol Lab collects, uses and protects your information and your child's information.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Prodigy Futbol Lab Pty Ltd ("PFL", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). By using pflab.com.au or contacting us, you agree to this policy.

What we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us, including:

  • Parent or guardian details: name, mobile number and email address.
  • Player details: name, age or date of birth, current or previous club, grading, playing position, preferred foot and Football Victoria (FFV) number.
  • Enquiry details: the programs you are interested in, how you heard about us and any notes you add.

Information about players is provided by their parent or guardian. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us for you.

How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and book sessions, trials and programs.
  • To send updates you have agreed to receive, such as trial dates, holiday clinics, programs and Youth World Cup information.
  • To match players with the right program and to run our sessions safely.
  • To improve our website and measure our advertising.

You can unsubscribe from updates at any time by replying to any message or contacting us below.

Website forms

Our website forms are delivered to us by email through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-delivery service. The booking form also opens a text message to Coach Tom on your own device, which you choose whether to send.

Cookies and the Meta Pixel

We use the Meta Pixel, a tool from Meta (Facebook and Instagram), to understand how visitors use our site and to measure and improve our ads. It uses cookies and records actions such as page views and taps on our contact buttons. Meta may match this activity to your Meta account in a hashed (encrypted) form. We do not send children's names or dates of birth to Meta.

You can control ad personalisation in your Meta ad preferences and block cookies in your browser settings. Read Meta's privacy policy.

Sharing your information

We never sell your information. We only share it with service providers who help us run PFL (such as email, messaging and website services), with Football Victoria or tournament organisers where needed to register a player you have entered, or where the law requires it. Some of these providers may store information outside Australia.

Photos and video

We may photograph or film sessions, matches and events for our website and social media. We only publish images of players where we have consent from a parent or guardian. You can ask us to remove an image at any time.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, and we only keep it for as long as we need it.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you or your child, or ask us to delete it. If you have a privacy concern, contact us and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
